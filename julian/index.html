📖 The Reading · Why English Opens Doors
Many Latino contractors work hard in the US. They do construction, painting and remodeling. But there is one big opportunity: English.

When a contractor speaks English, he talks to the client himself. He gives the estimate. He answers the questions. He is not waiting for another person to help.

Right now, many clients are looking for a good contractor. They want clear answers. English opens doors: new clients, bigger jobs, and his own business.

It is not easy. But every day, he practices a little. Every day, a door opens a little more.

Guided · The Shape
1st time · new · a / an
2nd time · we know which · the
I see a crack.
The crack is big.
She wants an estimate.
The estimate is $600.
I have a new client.
The client wants a floor.
First time it's new → a / an. After that you both know which one → the. (a / an = un / una · the = el / la)
a→ one thing, new, the first time. "a ladder, a client"
an→ the same, before a vowel sound. "an estimate, an apartment"
the→ you both know which one. "the client, the estimate"
nothing→ many, in general. "clients, materials, tiles"
500 or 600 include material.
It's 500 or 600, and that includes the material. — you don't hear the little word, so you don't say it. Now you know it's there.
Bring the ladder. (nobody knows which ladder yet)
We need a ladder. The ladder is in the truck. — new first, then "the".
