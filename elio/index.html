Class 1
Job Identity: answer "what's your job / who do you work for" without freezing
By the end of this class, you can answer "What's your job?" and "Who do you work for?" out loud, confidently, using a fixed frame — no freezing, no "I don't know."

Meet Fix
Answer fast, no thinking time
"What's your job?"
Cue if you freeze: "I'm a(n)___" — say the whole sentence, not just the missing word.
I'm an electrician.
A full sentence beats a single word.

CORE · Round 1 — Build It
A new coworker introduces themselves and asks what you do. Use your 3 frames to answer.
Take your time. Use "I'm a(n)___" and "I work for ___" at least once each.
CORE · Round 2 — Same Frames, Faster
Same introduction, same frames — just less thinking time between them.
Not new content, just more comfort and speed with what you already said.

Real-Life Conversation
A new coworker introduces themselves on the job site and asks what you do.
Coworker: Hey, I'm Mike. I just started here. What's your job?
You: Nice to meet you. I'm an electrician. I work for [boss's name].
Coworker: How long have you been doing this?
You: I've been doing this for [number] years. That's right — mostly wiring and panels.
