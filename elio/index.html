How'd It Go? — real-world debrief + consolidation
By the end of this class, you can tell someone about a real conversation you had in English, connect two ideas with "but," and use three new phrases you already met — "it's not a big deal," "just in case," "pretty" (= very) — using fixed frames.
Open With A Win

Your real life since last class — that's what today runs on.
Critical Thinking · 1 of 3

Critical Thinking · 2 of 3

Critical Thinking · 3 of 3

Reading · How'd It Go?
Listen once. Then reveal the text and read it twice — the second time, just catch the past-tense verbs and the three new phrases.
Last weekend I talked with my girlfriend about my day. I didn't know one word. I was nervous.

She said, "It's not a big deal — just say it in Spanish and I'll tell you the word." I said, "Okay." She helped me.

Later, I said, "I'll take my notebook, just in case I forget something." She said, "Good idea."

It was a pretty long day. But I had a good time.
Catch these: talked · didn't know · was · said · helped · was
