Real-Life Conversation
A new coworker asks you to describe your role on your first day.
Greet · say what you do · use "to be honest" · handle a follow-up question
Coworker: So what exactly do you do here?
You: I'm a phlebotomist — I draw blood and process specimens for the lab. To be honest, the biggest part of my job is talking to patients, not just the blood draw.
Coworker: Is it always this busy?
You: It depends on the day. Mornings are usually the busiest.
